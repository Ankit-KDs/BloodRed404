Donor Eligibility Check

Not sure if you are eligible to donate today? Take our quick interactive health screening to instantly verify your status based on age, weight, and recent medical history.

Question 1 of 5 20% Completed
Step 1: Age Criteria

Are you between 18 and 65 years old?

National clinical blood banking regulations mandate voluntary blood donors to be at least 18 years of age.

Step 2: Body Weight

Do you weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs)?

Adequate body weight ensures safe physiological blood volume extraction (standard 350ml or 450ml draw) without vasovagal dizziness.

Step 3: Donation Interval

Has it been more than 90 days since your last whole blood donation?

First-time donors can select 'Yes'. Erythrocyte red cell replenishment requires an obligatory 3-month biological recovery cycle.

Step 4: Health Status Today

Are you feeling fully well today, free of fever or active antibiotics?

Donors should be asymptomatic and not currently taking antibiotics for active systemic infection.

Step 5: Recent Procedures

Have you had any tattoos, piercings, or major surgeries in the past 6 months?

A 6-month deferral window protects transfusion recipients from potential latent blood-borne transmission.
