Frequently Asked Questions

Last Updated: November 2026

1. What is RED404?

RED404 is an emergency blood rescue network connecting voluntary blood donors directly with accredited hospital blood banks and patients facing critical shortages.

2. How does Public Donor Search work?

Your first name, last initial, blood group, approximate distance and number of donations appear in RED404's public donor search. Hospitals see your phone number only after you accept a request. You can hide yourself any time by pausing your availability.

To protect donor privacy, exact coordinates and addresses are never shared. Distances are computed using privacy-preserving 1 km grid rounding.

3. Who can see my phone number and contact details?

Your phone number is strictly private. It is never displayed on public search results or directories. Only verified hospital clinical teams receive your contact number, and only after you have explicitly accepted an active emergency blood request.

4. How do I pause my availability if I cannot donate?

You can pause your availability at any time directly from your donor dashboard. Once paused, you immediately disappear from public search and will not receive hospital requests until you resume.

5. How often can I donate blood?

In accordance with standard medical guidelines, whole blood donation recovery intervals are 90 days for male donors and 120 days for female donors. Your personal Medical Eligibility Tracker displays your recovery countdown in real time.

6. How does in-person hospital check-in work?

Present your Digital Blood Donor ID or scan your secure QR code at the hospital check-in counter. The QR code contains an opaque security token that allows medical staff to verify your eligibility and record vitals without exposing personal credentials.
