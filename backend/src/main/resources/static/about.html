ABOUT THE COMPANY

The Real-Time Response Network
For Life-Saving Emergencies.

We are engineering the future of emergency healthcare logistics. RED404 is a spatial-intelligence platform built to eliminate critical delays in blood procurement. By directly connecting verified hospitals with nearby eligible donors, we ensure that no medical crisis is ever lost to time.

1. Precision & Speed

Hyper-Local Spatial Routing

Time is the ultimate currency in a crisis. Our proprietary routing engine instantly maps the closest available donors within a dynamic 5km to 20km radius. When a hospital triggers an SOS, only the most immediate, highly-matched individuals are alerted—cutting response times from hours to mere minutes.

2. Total Data Security

Zero-Knowledge Identity Protection

Medical privacy is non-negotiable. Every user is assigned a secure, mathematically generated alphanumeric ID upon onboarding. Our strict 'Burned ID' architecture ensures that if a user leaves the network, their digital footprint is permanently retired. No real names on the live map, no data leaks, absolute anonymity.

3. Seamless Infrastructure

Direct Hospital Integration

We empower medical frontline workers with direct-access portals to broadcast high-priority blood requests instantly. The platform handles the complex logistics—from real-time donor matching to automated navigation routing—allowing healthcare professionals to focus entirely on saving lives.
