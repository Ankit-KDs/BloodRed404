ABOUT THE COMPANY

The Real-Time Response Network
For Life-Saving Emergencies.

We are engineering the future of emergency healthcare logistics. RED404 is a real-time emergency blood network built to eliminate critical delays in blood procurement. By directly connecting verified hospitals with nearby eligible donors, we ensure that no medical crisis is ever lost to time.

1. Precision & Speed

Fast Nearby Donor Matching

Time is the ultimate currency in a crisis. Our network instantly locates eligible donors within a 5km to 20km radius. When a hospital triggers an emergency request, only the closest, compatible individuals are notified—cutting response times from hours to mere minutes.

2. Total Data Security

Complete Privacy & Identity Protection

Medical privacy is non-negotiable. Every donor is assigned a secure digital ID upon onboarding. When an account is deleted, personal records are permanently erased from the system. No phone numbers are revealed without explicit request acceptance, ensuring absolute privacy.

3. Seamless Infrastructure

Direct Hospital Integration

We empower medical frontline workers with direct-access portals to broadcast high-priority blood requests instantly. The platform handles the complex logistics—from real-time donor matching to automated navigation routing—allowing healthcare professionals to focus entirely on saving lives.
