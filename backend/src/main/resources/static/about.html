Helping hospitals find blood donors nearby

RED404 is a voluntary community project that connects registered blood donors with verified hospitals in the same area. When a hospital needs blood for a patient, it can reach eligible donors nearby in minutes instead of calling people one by one.

Why we built it

Finding the right blood group in time often depends on phone calls, messages and luck. Donors want to help but rarely know when they are needed. Hospitals know what they need but cannot see who is nearby and able to donate. RED404 puts both sides in one place.

For donors

You register with your blood group and location. A hospital confirms your details when you visit. You are notified only when your blood group is needed nearby and you are eligible. You can accept or decline, get directions, and pause your availability whenever you like. After a donation you see it in your history, can download a certificate and see when you can donate again.

For hospitals

A hospital registers and is verified by our team before it can send requests. You keep your blood stock up to date, search for eligible donors by blood group and distance, send a request, and record each donation when the donor arrives.

Verified by whom?

Hospitals are reviewed and verified by our platform team before they can broadcast requests.

Your privacy

Hospitals see your phone number only after you accept their request. Public pages never show your phone, address or exact location. Donors appear in public search only if they choose to. Your location is used only to find requests near you. You can ask us to delete your account.

Safety and medical decisions

Basic eligibility guidelines (age, weight, hemoglobin and the gap between donations) are shown on the site. They are a guide, not medical advice. A hospital doctor makes the final decision, and health measurements come from the hospital, not from the donor.

What RED404 is not

RED404 is not an ambulance or emergency service. In a medical emergency call 112. It is not a blood bank itself; stock figures are entered by each hospital and may be a few minutes old.

Questions or feedback

If you have questions, feedback or need assistance, visit our Contact section or FAQ, or email us at support@red404.org.
