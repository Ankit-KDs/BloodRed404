Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Data Minimisation

RED404 is an emergency blood rescue network designed to connect volunteer blood donors with verified hospital facilities. We adhere strictly to data minimisation principles, collecting only information required to facilitate life-saving donations.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Volunteer Donors: Name, mobile number (+91 format checked, not verified), verified email address, date of birth, gender, self-declared blood type, self-declared health questionnaire answers, and approximate locality (city, state, PIN code). Exact street addresses are never collected.
  • Hospitals & Blood Centres: Facility name, official registration and blood bank licence details, authorised coordinator contact information, facility coordinates, operating hours, and compliance verification documents.

3. Who Can See What

  • Donors: You can only view and manage your own profile, eligibility tracker, and donation history.
  • Hospitals: Verified hospitals see donor names, blood groups, and approximate distance when searching for emergency donors. Phone numbers are revealed to hospital staff only after a donor accepts an emergency request. Detailed health questionnaire responses are viewable exclusively by hospital clinical staff during in-person check-in.
  • Admins: Network administrators review hospital verification documents and maintain directory records. Admins never view donor health questionnaires or account passwords.
  • Public: Your first name, last initial, blood group, approximate distance and number of donations appear in RED404's public donor search. Hospitals see your phone number only after you accept a request. You can hide yourself any time by pausing your availability.

4. Application-Level Data Encryption

Donor health questionnaire answers are encrypted at rest at the application level using AES-256-GCM. Hospital verification documents are stored in isolated private storage and accessible to authorized reviewers only via short-lived, time-limited cryptographic tokens.

5. Your Rights as a Donor

  • Download My Data: You have the right to export a complete copy of your profile, consent logs, health questionnaire answers, and donation history in JSON format at any time from your settings.
  • Delete My Account: You can request immediate account deletion from your profile. Upon deletion, your personal identifying data (name, email, phone, location, health questionnaire) is permanently scrubbed and anonymised. Clinical donation records are preserved in anonymised format without linkage to your identity to maintain hospital regulatory records.

6. Hospital Account Lifecycle

Hospital registrations undergo human review before activation. Once verified, account deletion or status changes (such as suspension) are managed by network administrators via soft-deletion to ensure traceability and audit compliance.

7. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact our privacy desk at privacy@red404.org.
