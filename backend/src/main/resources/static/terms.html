Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Purpose of the Platform

RED404 operates as an emergency dispatch routing utility that facilitates contact between volunteer donors and healthcare institutions during critical blood shortages.

2. Medical Disclaimer

RED404 is a routing utility and does not provide medical services, clinical diagnosis, or phlebotomy. All self-declared eligibility details and blood groups are preliminary. The final decision regarding donor suitability and blood safety rests entirely with the certified clinical team at the participating hospital.

3. Donor Commitments

  • You confirm that you are at least 18 years of age and under 65 years of age.
  • You agree to provide truthful self-reported information on your health questionnaire.
  • Blood donation is voluntary; you will never be charged or compensated through the RED404 platform.

4. Hospital Commitments

  • Participating hospitals must possess valid government registration and authorized blood bank licensing where blood collection occurs.
  • All clinical donor checks, screening, and transfusion safety protocols must strictly adhere to regulatory medical standards.

5. Termination & Suspension

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate our safety policies or submit falsified documentation.
